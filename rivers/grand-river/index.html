Grand River fishing spots

The Grand River changes character along its length, and so do the rules. Here are the public places on each section we cover:

Tailwater: Shand Dam to West Montrose

Wild brown trout below Shand Dam, special catch-and-release stretches through Fergus and Elora.

  • Public access: Elora Gorge Conservation Area, Belwood Lake Conservation Area (Shand Dam) and West Montrose
  • Fish: Brown trout and Rainbow trout

Lower: Caledonia to Lake Erie (Dunnville)

Lake Erie steelhead and lake-run browns from Caledonia to Dunnville, open to December 31 below Paris.

  • Public access: Caledonia dam and Byng Island Conservation Area (Dunnville)
  • Fish: Steelhead and Brown trout (lake-run)

Grand River water temperature

Water temperature decides where trout and salmon hold and how hard they feed. Ontario's river gauges report level and flow, but very few report temperature, so Muddy York Fishing estimates the Grand River's water temperature every morning from live air temperature, recent weather and the river's flow. Members can add a thermometer reading when they log a trip, which keeps the estimate honest.

Water tempWhat the fish do
Below 4°CSlow. Fish deep, slow pools at midday with small flies.
4 to 10°CCool. Steelhead and browns feed, mostly near the bottom.
10 to 18°CPrime. Trout feed actively; hatches and dries come into play.
18 to 20°CWarm. Fish early, play fish fast, keep them in the water.
Above 20°CToo warm. Trout are stressed; leave them be or find colder water.

Fly fishing the Grand River

  • Brown trout: Pheasant Tail and Hare's Ear nymphs, Elk Hair Caddis and mayfly dries, and sculpin or Woolly Bugger streamers at dawn and dusk.
  • Rainbow trout: Pheasant Tail and Hare's Ear nymphs, Elk Hair Caddis and Adams dries.
  • Steelhead: egg patterns (Nuke Eggs, Sucker Spawn), stonefly and Pheasant Tail nymphs, and Woolly Buggers or Intruder style streamers.
  • Brown trout (lake-run): egg patterns and sculpin or minnow streamers.

On spinning gear: brown trout, small inline spinners and minnow imitating plugs cast tight to cover; rainbow trout, small inline spinners and spoons; steelhead, roe bags or soft beads under a float, plus small spinners and spoons in stained water. Hook and bait rules vary by stretch.

Grand River seasons by section

Ontario sets the season stretch by stretch, so the Grand River can be open in one place and closed a few kilometres away. From the Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary:

  • Tailwater: Shand Dam to West Montrose: Brown trout: fourth Saturday in April to September 30; Rainbow trout: fourth Saturday in April to September 30.
  • Lower: Caledonia to Lake Erie (Dunnville): Rainbow trout: open from fourth Saturday in April to December 31 (extended fall season); Brown trout: open from fourth Saturday in April to December 31 (extended fall season).

Each section page lists every stretch, its limits and any sanctuaries, with links to the official wording.

Before you fish the Grand River

  • Carry your Outdoors Card and fishing licence.
  • Check which section you'll fish and its season above. Rules often change at a bridge, dam or road.
  • Look at the level and flow at the top of this page. After heavy rain the Grand River can rise fast and be dangerous to wade.
  • Above about 20°C water, trout and salmon struggle. Fish early, or rest them.
  • Pack out everything you bring, including line and bait containers.

The Grand River Conservation Authority manages the Grand River watershed and posts flood and safety messages at grandriver.ca.

Frequently asked

Where are the best fishing spots on the Grand River?

Tailwater: Shand Dam to West Montrose: Elora Gorge Conservation Area, Belwood Lake Conservation Area (Shand Dam) and West Montrose. Lower: Caledonia to Lake Erie (Dunnville): Caledonia dam and Byng Island Conservation Area (Dunnville). Fish only where access is public, and check the stretch rules first.

What is the Grand River water level today?

The live readings on this page come from Water Survey of Canada gauges on the Grand River itself, one for each section. A rising level means fresh water and moving fish; the drop and clear a day or two later is usually the best fishing.

What is the water temperature of the Grand River?

It changes by the day and by stretch. Muddy York Fishing estimates it every morning from live air temperature, weather and flow. Trout feed best between about 10 and 18°C and struggle above 20°C.

Can you fly fish the Grand River?

Yes. The Grand River is a well-known fly fishing river for brown trout, rainbow trout and steelhead. Gear and bait rules differ by stretch, so check the section pages.

Is the Grand River open for fishing?

Each section has its own official season. The status chips on this page are checked against the Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary twice a day.
