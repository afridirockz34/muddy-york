How the Conestogo River fishes

Below the dam, flows and water temperature stay steadier than on a freestone river. The Conestogo River often fishes when nearby rivers are blown out or too warm, and cold releases can hold trout through summer. Nymphs work most of the year; dries and streamers come into play in prime temperatures and low light.

Tactics by species

  • Brown trout: nymph the seams in cool water, run a dry-dropper in prime temperatures, and swing or strip streamers at dawn and dusk for bigger fish.
  • Rainbow trout: nymph the seams and riffle tails, and run a dry-dropper over the riffles when fish are rising.

Where to fish the Conestogo River

Public places along this section include Conestogo Lake Conservation Area. Park in public lots, follow posted signs, and never cross private land without permission. Muddy York Fishing members get parking and walk-in routes for each section, and exact spots are never shared publicly.

Before you go

  • Carry your Outdoors Card and fishing licence.
  • Check which stretch you'll fish and its official season above. Rules often change at a bridge or road.
  • Look at the water temperature. Above about 20°C, trout and salmon struggle, so fish early or rest them.
  • After heavy rain the Conestogo River can be high and dangerous. Never wade water you can't read, and wear a wading belt.
  • Pack out everything you bring, including line and bait containers.

Frequently asked

What fish are in the Conestogo River?

The Conestogo River (Tailwater: below Conestogo Dam) holds Brown trout and Rainbow trout. Activity peaks for brown trout in Apr, May, Jun, Sep and Oct and rainbow trout in Apr, May, Sep and Oct.

Is the Conestogo River good for fishing?

Cold tailwater below the Conestogo Dam with brown and rainbow trout near Waterloo. Like most Southern Ontario rivers it fishes best on the right day: steady, cool water and low light are prime, while warm summer afternoons are poor.

When is the best time to fish the Conestogo River?

Peak months are Apr, May, Jun, Sep and Oct for brown trout and Apr, May, Sep and Oct for rainbow trout. Within those months, cool cloudy days and the first and last light are best.

Where can I fish the Conestogo River?

Do I need a fishing licence?

Yes, most anglers need an Outdoors Card and a valid Ontario fishing licence. This river is in Fisheries Management Zone 16.
