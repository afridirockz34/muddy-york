Can I fish the Niagara River right now?

Ontario sets the season by stretch. Here are the official rules for this section (Fisheries Management Zone 20), from the Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary:

Below the Falls (Whirlpool, Devil's Hole)

The Niagara River downstream of the falls is in Zone 20.

  • Rainbow trout: open all year (limits S-2 and C-1)
  • Brown trout: open all year (limits S-5 and C-2)
  • Lake trout: January 1 to September 30 and December 1 to December 31 (limits S-3 and C-1)

How the Niagara River fishes

These cold, spring-fed headwaters run clear and cool. Trout hold in shade, undercut banks and pocket water. Approach quietly, use light tippet, and fish small dries, dry-droppers and nymphs.

Tactics by species

  • Steelhead: drift eggs, stoneflies and nymphs through runs and tailouts under a float or on a tight line, and swing streamers when the water is stained.
  • Brown trout: nymph the seams in cool water, run a dry-dropper in prime temperatures, and swing or strip streamers at dawn and dusk for bigger fish.
  • Lake trout: work deep, slow streamers and jigs off the main current in the cold months.

Where to fish the Niagara River

Public places along this section include Niagara Glen, Whirlpool and Devil's Hole. Park in public lots, follow posted signs, and never cross private land without permission. Muddy York Fishing members get parking and walk-in routes for each section, and exact spots are never shared publicly.

Before you go

  • Carry your Outdoors Card and fishing licence.
  • Check which stretch you'll fish and its official season above. Rules often change at a bridge or road.
  • Look at the water temperature. Above about 20°C, trout and salmon struggle, so fish early or rest them.
  • After heavy rain the Niagara River can be high and dangerous. Never wade water you can't read, and wear a wading belt.
  • Pack out everything you bring, including line and bait containers.

Frequently asked

What fish are in the Niagara River?

The Niagara River (Lower: Whirlpool / Devil's Hole drifts) holds Steelhead, Brown trout and Lake trout. Activity peaks for steelhead in Mar, Apr and Nov and brown trout in Apr, May, Jun, Sep and Oct.

Is the Niagara River good for fishing?

Big-water drifts for steelhead, brown trout and lake trout below the falls, in Fisheries Management Zone 20. Like most Southern Ontario rivers it fishes best on the right day: fresh rain pulls new fish in from the lake, and the drop and clear afterwards is prime.

Can I fish the Niagara River right now?

When is the best time to fish the Niagara River?

Peak months are Mar, Apr and Nov for steelhead and Apr, May, Jun, Sep and Oct for brown trout. Within those months, cool cloudy days and the first and last light are best.

Where can I fish the Niagara River?

Public places along this section include Niagara Glen, Whirlpool and Devil's Hole. Only fish where access is public or permitted, respect posted private land, and check the stretch rules above.

Do I need a fishing licence?

Yes, most anglers need an Outdoors Card and a valid Ontario fishing licence. This river is in Fisheries Management Zone 20.
