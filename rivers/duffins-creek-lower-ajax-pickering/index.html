How the Duffins Creek fishes

This is migratory water. Steelhead and salmon move up from the lake on fresh rain, so the best fishing usually comes as the river drops and clears a day or two after a rise. Fish hold in deep pools, tailouts and current seams. Drift eggs and nymphs, or swing and strip streamers, depending on clarity.

Tactics by species

  • Steelhead: drift eggs, stoneflies and nymphs through runs and tailouts under a float or on a tight line, and swing streamers when the water is stained.
  • Chinook salmon: fish deep holding pools and log-jam tailouts in the lower river with bright streamers swung slowly, or drift roe and egg patterns. Use stout tackle.
  • Atlantic salmon (restoration): Atlantic salmon are catch-and-release only. Use small wets and nymphs on light tippet in low light, and release them quickly.

Where to fish the Duffins Creek

Public places along this section include Rotary Park (Ajax) and Greenwood Conservation Area. Park in public lots, follow posted signs, and never cross private land without permission. Muddy York Fishing members get parking and walk-in routes for each section, and exact spots are never shared publicly.

Before you go

  • Carry your Outdoors Card and fishing licence.
  • Check which stretch you'll fish and its official season above. Rules often change at a bridge or road.
  • Look at the water temperature. Above about 20°C, trout and salmon struggle, so fish early or rest them.
  • After heavy rain the Duffins Creek can be high and dangerous. Never wade water you can't read, and wear a wading belt.
  • Pack out everything you bring, including line and bait containers.

Frequently asked

What fish are in the Duffins Creek?

The Duffins Creek (Lower: Ajax / Pickering) holds Steelhead, Chinook salmon and Atlantic salmon (restoration). Activity peaks for steelhead in Mar, Apr and Nov and chinook salmon in Sep.

Is the Duffins Creek good for fishing?

Chinook, steelhead and Atlantic salmon in Ajax and Pickering. Open all year below the CNR tracks. Like most Southern Ontario rivers it fishes best on the right day: fresh rain pulls new fish in from the lake, and the drop and clear afterwards is prime.

Can I fish the Duffins Creek right now?

It depends on the stretch: CNR tracks to Lake Ontario, Hwy 2 to the CNR tracks, Above Hwy 2 each have their own official rules, listed on this page. The status at the top of the page is checked against the Ontario summary twice a day.

When is the salmon run on the Duffins Creek?

Chinook salmon usually enter the Duffins Creek from late August, peak in September and finish in October. Each fresh rain brings a new push of fish.

Where can I fish the Duffins Creek?

Public places along this section include Rotary Park (Ajax) and Greenwood Conservation Area. Only fish where access is public or permitted, respect posted private land, and check the stretch rules above.

Do I need a fishing licence?

Yes, most anglers need an Outdoors Card and a valid Ontario fishing licence. This river is in Fisheries Management Zone 17.
