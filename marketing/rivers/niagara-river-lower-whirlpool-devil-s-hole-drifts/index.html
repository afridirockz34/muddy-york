Overview

Enormous, oxygen-rich cold river holding steelhead, big browns and lake trout. Peaks late fall through spring; deep and fishable but slower in high summer.

What to expect

The Niagara River (Lower — Whirlpool / Devil's Hole drifts) is massive cold tailrace from lake erie in Niagara. Anglers here target Steelhead, Brown trout and Lake trout. Conditions swing with rainfall, water temperature and the seasonal runs — which is exactly what Muddy York Fishing tracks for you day by day.
