## Voice Pillars

| Pillar | What it means | Example | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct | Lead with the answer | "Chinook run the Ganaraska from late August, peaking in September." | Throat-clearing intros, "In this article we will" |
| Local and specific | Real river, road and town names | "Below the Highway 403 bridge the Credit is open all year." | Generic lines that fit any river |
| Honest | No promises about catching fish | "Fresh rain pulls new fish in; warm, dry spells stall the run." | "Guaranteed", "never get skunked", "the best" without proof |
| Respectful of the resource | Conservation and the rules come first | "Spawning fish need room: watch from the bank, don't wade the redds." | Encouraging fishing closed water or warm-water trout |

## Terminology: Say This, Not That

| Say this | Not that | Why |
|---|---|---|
| fish sanctuary, closed to fishing | "off limits", "banned" | Official wording |
| Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary | "the regs book" | Official name on first mention |
| Fisheries Management Zone 16 (FMZ 16) | "area 16" | Official term |
| steelhead (lake-run rainbow trout) | "rainbows" for lake-run fish | Precision |
| public access | "secret spot" | Privacy and landowner respect |

## Words and Phrases to Avoid Entirely

- Em dashes (—) and en dashes used as punctuation in copy
- guaranteed
- never get skunked
- secret spot
- hidden gem
- unfair advantage
- game-changer
- unlock the secrets
- In this article
- In today's fast-paced world

## Sensitivities

- Never publish or imply exact private spots or GPS.
- Don't encourage fishing closed seasons, sanctuaries, spawning redds, or warm water (20°C+).
- Regulation statements must come from the official summary data; anything not covered says "check the official regulations" with a link.
- Respect private land and Indigenous land access; only name public places (parks, conservation areas, public fishways).
- No guaranteed-catch claims.
