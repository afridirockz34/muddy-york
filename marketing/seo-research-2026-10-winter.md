River steelhead searches are real but small (each river 50 to 160 a winter).
Winter search in Ontario is overwhelmingly **ice fishing**, centred on Lake
Simcoe, then Nipissing, Quinte, Rice, Scugog, Simcoe's Cook's Bay and
Kempenfelt Bay. "Ice conditions" alone is 13,500 searches: people want to
know if it's safe and fishable today. That is exactly the kind of daily,
live, local question the app answers for rivers.

The other big winter traffic is the **fishing licence** (63,500), which every
angler searches before the season, and the **Toronto Sportsmen's Show** in March.

## Recommendation

1. **Decide on ice fishing (the big one).** Adding Lake Simcoe and 5 to 6
   ice-fishing lakes to the app (species by season, official zone rules, the
   hut operators, weather and freeze conditions) would multiply winter
   traffic many times over. It's a product change, not just SEO, so it needs
   your decision. Ice safety must stay with official sources and operators;
   we would never say ice is safe.
2. **Fishing licence guide** (do now, all-year traffic): "Ontario fishing
   licence 2027: cost, who needs one, Outdoors Card and free fishing weekends",
   with links to the province. Huge volume, easy to rank with plain answers,
   and every reader is about to go fishing.
3. **Winter river pages** (built, live after push): the 18 steelhead pages
   and the winter steelhead post. Add "ice-free winter fishing near Toronto"
   to catch people who don't want ice.
4. **Seasonal landing pages**, live by mid-November:
   - "Ice fishing Lake Simcoe 2027" hub (if we do ice fishing), plus Nipissing,
     Quinte, Rice Lake, Scugog, Kempenfelt Bay pages.
   - "Ice conditions Lake Simcoe" page linking official ice and weather
     sources and operators' updates, refreshed daily from weather data.
   - "Fishing gifts for Ontario anglers" (December) leading to a **gift
     membership**, which needs a small Stripe gift checkout.
   - "Toronto Sportsmen's Show 2027 guide" (published January, peaks March).
   - "Family Day free fishing weekend 2027" (February).
5. **Timing.** Publish by mid-November so pages are indexed before the
   January peak; refresh dates every year.

## How we'll know it's working

Search Console impressions for "ice fishing", "licence" and "steelhead"
queries from November, sign-ups by landing page in the admin, and ChatGPT
referrals (utm_source=chatgpt.com) per page.
