## Body

When the rivers slow down, a lot of you head for the ice. So the app now has a second mode for winter.

**[IMAGE: ice-3-steps.jpg, or ice.gif]**
Alt text: Three phone screens. The new Ice fishing tab, a list of lakes with freeze progress, then Lake Simcoe's open species and rules.

Here's how it works:

1. **Switch to Ice fishing.** When ice season starts, an Ice fishing tab appears above your rivers.
2. **See how far each lake has frozen.** We add up this winter's freezing weather (freezing degree-days) for each lake. The app tells you when it's "Not cold enough yet", when the "Ice building" stage starts and when there's been "Established cold". Once a lake has had enough freezing weather, it also gets an outlook score for the day.
3. **Check what's open.** Each lake lists the fish open today and what's closed, from the Ontario regulations, with a link to the official rules and directions.

**We can't see the ice.** Freeze progress is weather, not an ice report. Check thickness yourself as you go, ask hut operators and locals, and follow Canadian Red Cross and Lifesaving Society guidance.
