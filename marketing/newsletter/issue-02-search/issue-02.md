## Body

Until today, finding one particular river in the app meant scrolling the list or panning the map. Now there's a search.

**[IMAGE: search-3-steps.jpg, or search.gif]**
Alt text: Three phone screens. The search icon next to the bell, a search for "credit" listing every Credit River stretch, then the Lower Credit's report at 68.

Here's how it works:

1. **Tap the search icon.** It sits next to the bell at the top of the app, wherever you are.
2. **Type a river.** Type "credit" and the Credit River's three main stretches come first, with today's score. Below them is every other spot we've mapped along the river, by the nearest town.
3. **Pick your stretch.** Its full report opens, just like a saved river: score, season, water, weather and the live gauge. Tap the star to save it.

It covers every named river and creek we've mapped in Southern Ontario, not only the main stretches.
