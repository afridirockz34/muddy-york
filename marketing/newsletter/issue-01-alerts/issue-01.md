## Body

Friday morning, Wilmot Creek in Newcastle scored 73 out of 100. The sky was overcast, the water was 10°C and the stretch was in season. If you'd saved Wilmot and set your alert to 70, your phone would have buzzed around eleven.

That's the feature this week: **prime-condition alerts.**

[IMAGE: alerts-3-steps.jpg, or alerts.gif]
Alt text: Three phone screens. An alert reading "Prime conditions on Wilmot Creek", then the river's report at 73, then today's flies.

Here's how it works:

1. **Your phone buzzes.** Save the rivers you fish. Every hour we check them against live water temperature, flow and weather. When one reaches the level you set, you hear about it.
2. **Tap it, and the river's report opens.** You see the score, today's season on that stretch, the conditions and the live gauge, all on one screen.
3. **Tie on the right fly.** You get today's approach and the patterns for that water. Then tap Directions.

We never alert you about water that's closed. You get at most two alerts a week per river, and never more than one message every 12 hours. You hear about the good days, not every day.
