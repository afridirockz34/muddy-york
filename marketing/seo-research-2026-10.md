### Why clicks are low
1. **Titles are 95 to 110 characters.** Google shows about 60 on mobile, so searchers
   see "Duffins Creek Fishing (Lower — Ajax / Pick…" and nothing else.
2. **Titles don't use the searchers' words.** People search for "salmon run",
   "fishing spots", town names, species and the year. The titles use section names
   ("Lower — Twyn Rivers to the mouth") and "Conditions & Species".
3. **Every meta description uses one template** with no reason to pick our result.

## 3. Who we're up against (Google.ca mobile, top 10 for 38 searches)

- **Page one is weak.** Facebook (41 appearances), Reddit (36), YouTube (28) and
  Instagram (12) hold most spots, plus fishing forums. Few real content pages rank,
  so well-made pages can move up quickly.
- **The real competitors are Ontario sites**, not the US apps in the questionnaire:
  - **ontariotroutandsteelhead.com** (a guide's blog, 1,081 ranking searches): wins
    "salmon run" (2,900), "credit river" (4,400), "fishing near toronto". Long,
    question-led articles.
  - **hookedontario.ca** (1,580 ranking searches): one page per water ("Duffins Creek
    Rotary Park Ajax" ranks #6 for "duffins creek ajax"), species pages, and
    "Ontario fishing zones explained" (#4 for "ontario fishing zones", 1,000/mo).
  - **whattofish.ca** (567 ranking searches): a page per zone ("zone 16 fishing
    regulations" #2, "zone 17" #5). We now hold better data for this: the official
    summary for all 20 zones, synced twice a day.
  - **fishangler.com:** an auto-generated page per water ("Fishing at Bowmanville
    Creek, Ontario").
- **Google features on these searches:** AI Overviews on most, "People also ask" on
  nearly all, video and local packs often. Clear question-and-answer sections and
  specific facts (dates, places, rules) are what get quoted.
- DataForSEO's index sees us ranking for only 4 searches (best: position 38 to 71),
  which matches the Search Console picture of a new site on the edge of page one.

## 4. What searchers ask ("People also ask")

- Are the salmon running in Ontario right now? When do salmon run in Ontario?
- Where is the best place to see the salmon run in Port Hope / Bowmanville / Mississauga?
- Where is the Bowmanville / Port Hope fish ladder?
- Is the Humber River open for salmon fishing? Can I fish the Credit River right now?
- What fish are in Duffins Creek / the Ganaraska / the Rouge / the Grand River?
- Is [river] good for fishing? Where to fish on the Saugeen River?
- What fish are in season right now in Ontario? Who can fish without a licence?
- When can I fish for steelhead in Ontario? What month is best for steelhead?
- Which fishing app is most accurate? Does Fishbrain work in Canada?

## 5. Recommendations, in order of impact

### A. Rewrite every title and meta description (fast, low risk)
- Keep titles under 60 characters, leading with the river and the searchers'
  words: "fishing", the town, "salmon run", "spots", and the year where it fits.
  Example: **"Duffins Creek Fishing, Ajax & Pickering | Salmon & Steelhead"**
- Descriptions of 140 to 155 characters with a concrete hook, for example today's
  season status, when the run peaks, or where to park, plus "Free 7-day trial".
- The H1 matches the title's intent; the section name moves to a subheading.
- Drop the em dashes from titles.

### B. Build a salmon run section before next August (biggest seasonal win)
- A hub: "Ontario Salmon Run 2027: Where and When to See It", with a live
  "are they running now?" answer drawn from the app's conditions.
- Pages for the high-demand spots: Port Hope fish ladder (Ganaraska), Bowmanville
  fish ladder, Humber River at Old Mill, Credit River (Mississauga / Streetsville),
  Bronte, Duffins (Ajax/Pickering). Each covers when, where to stand, parking and
  what's legal to fish (from the official regulations sync).
- Publish now so pages age into next season; refresh each August.

### C. Official regulations pages by zone (year round, our strongest asset)
- `/regulations/` plus `/regulations/zone-16/` and so on, generated from the official
  Ontario summary we already sync twice a day: seasons by species, plus river
  exceptions with deep links to ontario.ca.
- Targets "zone 16 fishing regulations" (880), "zone 17" (590), "ontario fishing zones"
  (1,000), "ontario fishing season" (720), and "trout / salmon / steelhead season
  ontario".

### D. Strengthen the river pages to move from positions 8 to 12 into the top 5
- Add a "What fish are in [river]" section, a "When to fish it" month-by-month
  table, a "Can I fish it right now?" line driven by the regulations data,
  "Where to fish & park" (public access only, never private spots) and a short FAQ
  matching the questions above.
- Link each river page to its salmon run page, zone page and region page.
- Answer the river-name searches that aren't about fishing ("duffins creek" →
  where it is, the trails, the parks) briefly, to capture that traffic.

### E. Town pages for "fishing spots in [town]"
Toronto, Hamilton, Mississauga, Oakville, Port Hope, Barrie, Pickering/Ajax,
Oshawa/Bowmanville, Owen Sound and others. Each lists the nearby rivers and links
to their pages. Programmatic, but each needs real local detail to rank.

### F. Landing page calls to action
- Above the fold on every river page: today's season status and condition from
  the app, then "See today's conditions: 7 days free". Repeat after the FAQ.
- Price shown per month ($5.00/mo yearly), "Cancel anytime", no card wording
  surprises.
- 75% of searchers are on phones: a sticky bottom call to action on mobile.

### G. Measurement
- Track landing page → sign-up → trial in the app's own analytics (already
  partly in place: first-touch source and landing page).
- Add the blog sitemap in Search Console; re-pull Search Console monthly.
