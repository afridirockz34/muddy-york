Ice fishing Bay of Quinte

Bay of Quinte is in Fisheries Management Zone 20. Here's what Ontario's records say lives there, what's open through the ice season under the official rules, and how cold this winter has been so far.

Ontario's lake records file the Bay of Quinte with Lake Ontario, so we list the bay's own ice-fishing species rather than all of Lake Ontario's, and don't show depths.

We can't see the ice

Nothing on this page tells you the ice is safe. Check thickness yourself as you go, ask the hut operators and locals, and follow ice safety guidance from the Canadian Red Cross and the Lifesaving Society.

Where anglers head

Common starting points on Bay of Quinte include Belleville, Trenton, Picton. Conditions differ a lot from bay to bay, so ask locally about the exact area you plan to fish.

Before you go out

  • Check the ice with a spud bar or auger as you walk, not just at the shore.
  • Stay away from pressure ridges, river mouths, bridges, docks and springs, where ice is often thinner.
  • Wear a flotation suit or PFD, carry ice picks and a throw rope, and never go alone.
  • Tell someone where you're going and when you'll be back.
  • Hut operators rent heated huts on many big lakes and know their ice; using one is the easiest way to start.

Frequently asked

When does ice fishing start on Bay of Quinte?

It depends on the weather each winter, not a date. Ice forms after a long run of freezing weather and is never uniform. Hut operators and local reports are the best guide; never go out on ice you haven't checked.

What can you catch ice fishing on Bay of Quinte?

Ontario's lake records list walleye, yellow perch, northern pike, crappie, lake whitefish in Bay of Quinte. Under the current rules, walleye, yellow perch, northern pike, crappie, lake whitefish can be fished in mid-winter.

What fishing zone is Bay of Quinte in?

Fisheries Management Zone 20.

Do I need a fishing licence to ice fish?

Yes, the same Outdoors Card and fishing licence as any other fishing in Ontario, unless you're exempt (for example under 18 or 65 and older) or it's a free fishing weekend.
