Ontario salmon run 2026: where and when to see it

Every fall, Chinook salmon swim out of the Great Lakes and up Southern Ontario's rivers to spawn, leaping fish ladders and weirs on the way. The run starts in late August, peaks in September and is over by early November.

Below are the best-known places to watch near Toronto and around Lake Ontario and Georgian Bay, with what's legal to fish at each, straight from the official Ontario regulations.

When is the salmon run in Ontario?

Chinook (king) salmon are the main event. They begin staging off river mouths in August, push upstream on the first cool rains of late August and September, and spawn through early October. Coho follow into October and November. Lake-run steelhead and brown trout often follow the salmon in, feeding on eggs.

The run comes in waves. A heavy rain raises and colours the rivers and pulls a fresh push of fish; a warm, dry spell stalls it. The day or two after rain is usually the best time to see fish moving.

Where to see the salmon run

Port Hope salmon run and fish ladder

The best-known viewing spot is the fish ladder at Corbett Dam on the Ganaraska River in Port Hope, where Chinook salmon leap the falls and climb the fishway. Bring patience: fish move in waves, often after rain.

Humber River salmon run at Old Mill

In Toronto, the classic place to watch is the Humber River at Old Mill and Étienne Brûlé Park, where salmon jump the weirs on their way upstream. It's an easy walk from Old Mill subway station.

River guide: Humber River fishing

Bowmanville salmon run and fish ladder

The fish ladder on Bowmanville Creek is one of the easiest places east of Toronto to see Chinook salmon jump. The Bowmanville Valley Trail follows the creek.

River guide: Bowmanville Creek fishing

Credit River salmon run in Mississauga

Erindale Park in Mississauga is a popular place to watch salmon move up the Credit, and Port Credit at the river mouth sees fish staging in early fall.

River guide: Credit River fishing

Duffins Creek salmon run in Ajax and Pickering

Rotary Park in Ajax and the trails along Duffins Creek are good places to look for Chinook salmon in September and October.

Owen Sound salmon run on the Sydenham River

Owen Sound's salmon gather below the Mill Dam fishway on the Sydenham River and in the river through Harrison Park, a short walk from downtown.

Watching the run responsibly

  • Watch from the bank and stay off the gravel beds where salmon are spawning.
  • Don't touch, chase or throw things at the fish.
  • Keep dogs leashed and away from the water's edge.
  • Fish only where it's open. Several popular viewing spots are sanctuaries during the run.

Frequently asked

When is the salmon run in Ontario?

Chinook salmon start entering Lake Ontario, Lake Huron and Georgian Bay tributaries in late August, peak through September and finish in October. Coho run a little later, into October and November. Exact timing shifts each year with rain and water temperature.

Are the salmon running right now?

In September and early October, almost certainly in the rivers listed on this page; by November the Chinook run is over. A cool, rainy week brings the biggest pushes. Muddy York Fishing tracks each river's conditions daily.

Where is the best place to see the salmon run near Toronto?

The Humber River at Old Mill in Toronto, the Credit River in Mississauga, the fish ladders in Port Hope and Bowmanville, and Duffins Creek in Ajax are all within an hour of downtown.

What time of day is best to see the salmon run?

Early morning and evening, and on overcast days after rain, when fish are most active and moving. Midday in bright sun is usually slowest.

Why can't you eat salmon after spawning?

Chinook and coho die after spawning, and their bodies break down as they near the end of the run, so fish deep into the run are poor to eat. Many anglers release coloured fish.

Can you fish for salmon during the run?

In many places, yes, but rules change by stretch. Some sections near fish ladders are sanctuaries during the run. Each spot page below lists the official rules.
