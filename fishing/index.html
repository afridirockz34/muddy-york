Questions

What is Muddy York Fishing?

An app for Southern Ontario trout and salmon anglers. Each morning it ranks rivers on live conditions, shows the official season for each stretch, and suggests the technique and flies for the day.

Where does it work?

Rivers and creeks across Southern Ontario, from the Lake Ontario and Lake Erie tributaries to Georgian Bay and Lake Huron. You can also scout new water anywhere within your chosen radius.

Is it for beginners?

Yes. It tells you where to go, when it's good and what to tie on in plain language, and gives experienced anglers the live numbers behind it.

How accurate are the seasons?

They come from the official Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary, checked twice a day, with a link to the exact rule. Always confirm before you fish.

Do you share my fishing spots?

Never. Your exact locations and notes stay private. Community activity is only shown at the river level.

How much is it?

$9.99 a month, or $5.00 a month on the yearly plan (billed $59.99 a year). Both start with 7 free days.
