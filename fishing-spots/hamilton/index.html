Fishing spots in Hamilton

From Hamilton you're within an easy drive of the lower Grand River at Caledonia and Dunnville, Bronte Creek in Burlington and Oakville, and the Niagara region's creeks. The lower Grand carries a steelhead run from Lake Erie each spring and fall.

Inland waters around Hamilton are in Fisheries Management Zone 16; Hamilton Harbour is in Zone 20.

Fishing near Hamilton through the year

Spring (March to May): spring steelhead in the lower Bronte Creek, Sixteen Mile Creek and Grand River. Trout season opens on most inland stretches on the fourth Saturday in April.

Summer (June to August): the cold water on the Credit River and Twelve Mile Creek holds trout through the heat. Fish early and late, and stop when the water passes about 20°C.

Fall (September to November): the Chinook salmon run on the Bronte Creek, Sixteen Mile Creek and Credit River peaks from mid September to mid October, with steelhead following into November.

Winter (December to February): the stretches that are open all year hold steelhead in the slow, deep pools. Mild days after a thaw are best.

Fishing near Hamilton: good to know

  • Rules change by stretch, often at a road or bridge. Check the official season before you cast.
  • Fish only where access is public or permitted, and respect posted private land.
  • Trout and salmon struggle in warm water. In summer heat, fish early or rest them.

Frequently asked

Where can I go fishing in Hamilton?

The closest rivers we cover are the Bronte Creek (20 km), the Sixteen Mile Creek (26 km) and the Grand River (29 km). Each guide lists public access and the official season for every stretch.

What fish can I catch near Hamilton?

Steelhead, Chinook salmon, Brown trout (lake-run), Atlantic salmon (restoration), Brown trout, Rainbow trout and Brook trout, depending on the river and the season.

Where can I see the salmon run near Hamilton?

Humber River salmon run at Old Mill, Credit River salmon run in Mississauga: Erindale and Port Credit and Oakville salmon run on Sixteen Mile Creek. The run peaks in September.

What fishing zone is Hamilton in?

Inland waters around Hamilton are in Fisheries Management Zone 16; Hamilton Harbour is in Zone 20.

Do I need a licence to fish here?

Yes, most anglers need an Outdoors Card and a valid Ontario fishing licence. Ontario residents under 18 or 65 and over have different rules; check the official licensing page.
