Frequently asked

Where can I go trout fishing near me?

Tap "Use my location" and this page sorts 30+ Southern Ontario trout, steelhead and salmon rivers by distance from you, with what each holds and whether it's open today. Your location stays on your phone.

Where can I fly fish near Toronto?

The Credit, Humber and Rouge are inside the GTA, Bronte and Sixteen Mile creeks are in Oakville, Duffins Creek is in Ajax and Pickering, and the Grand, Ganaraska and Nottawasaga are a short drive away.

What is the best time to go trout fishing near me?

Cool, cloudy days and the first and last light of the day, in water between about 10 and 18°C. For steelhead, the day or two after a rain as the river drops and clears.

Are these public fishing spots?

Each river page lists public access along the stretch we cover. Fish only where access is public or permitted, and respect posted private land.
