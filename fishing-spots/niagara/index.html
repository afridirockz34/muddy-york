Fishing spots in Niagara Falls and St. Catharines

The lower Niagara River below the falls, through the Whirlpool and Devil's Hole, is a big-water fishery for steelhead, brown trout and lake trout. Twelve Mile Creek in the Short Hills near St. Catharines is a quiet, cold trout stream by contrast.

The Niagara River below the falls is in Fisheries Management Zone 20; inland creeks are in Zone 16.

Fishing near Niagara Falls and St. Catharines: good to know

  • Rules change by stretch, often at a road or bridge. Check the official season before you cast.
  • Fish only where access is public or permitted, and respect posted private land.
  • Trout and salmon struggle in warm water. In summer heat, fish early or rest them.

Frequently asked

Where can I go fishing in Niagara Falls and St. Catharines?

The closest rivers we cover are the Twelve Mile Creek (8 km), the Niagara River (9 km) and the Bronte Creek (54 km). Each guide lists public access and the official season for every stretch.

What fish can I catch near Niagara Falls and St. Catharines?

Brook trout, Brown trout, Steelhead, Lake trout, Chinook salmon, Brown trout (lake-run) and Atlantic salmon (restoration), depending on the river and the season.

Where can I see the salmon run near Niagara Falls and St. Catharines?

Humber River salmon run at Old Mill and Credit River salmon run in Mississauga. The run peaks in September.

What fishing zone is Niagara Falls and St. Catharines in?

The Niagara River below the falls is in Fisheries Management Zone 20; inland creeks are in Zone 16.

Do I need a licence to fish here?

Yes, most anglers need an Outdoors Card and a valid Ontario fishing licence. Ontario residents under 18 or 65 and over have different rules; check the official licensing page.
