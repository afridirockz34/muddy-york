Fishing spots in Toronto

Toronto's rivers run right through the city: the Humber in the west end, the Don through the middle and the Rouge along the eastern edge in Rouge National Urban Park. Each fall Chinook salmon push up them from Lake Ontario, and the Humber at Old Mill is a popular place to watch the run.

Toronto's rivers are in Fisheries Management Zone 16. Lake Ontario and Toronto Harbour are in Zone 20.

Fishing near Toronto: good to know

  • Rules change by stretch, often at a road or bridge. Check the official season before you cast.
  • Fish only where access is public or permitted, and respect posted private land.
  • Trout and salmon struggle in warm water. In summer heat, fish early or rest them.

Frequently asked

Where can I go fishing in Toronto?

The closest rivers we cover are the Humber River (9 km), the Rouge River (26 km) and the Credit River (28 km). Each guide lists public access and the official season for every stretch.

What fish can I catch near Toronto?

Steelhead, Chinook salmon, Atlantic salmon (restoration), Brown trout (lake-run), Brown trout, Rainbow trout and Brook trout, depending on the river and the season.

Where can I see the salmon run near Toronto?

Humber River salmon run at Old Mill, Credit River salmon run in Mississauga and Duffins Creek salmon run in Ajax and Pickering. The run peaks in September.

What fishing zone is Toronto in?

Toronto's rivers are in Fisheries Management Zone 16. Lake Ontario and Toronto Harbour are in Zone 20.

Do I need a licence to fish here?

Yes, most anglers need an Outdoors Card and a valid Ontario fishing licence. Ontario residents under 18 or 65 and over have different rules; check the official licensing page.
