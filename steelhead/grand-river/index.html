Grand River steelhead fishing

Lake Erie tributary · FMZ 16
Checking today's steelhead season… Lower: Caledonia to Lake Erie (Dunnville)

The Grand River gets Lake Erie steelhead in fall and again in spring. Lake Erie steelhead and lake-run browns from Caledonia to Dunnville, open to December 31 below Paris.

Below: when the fish are in, exactly which stretches are open in fall, winter and spring, and how to fish them.

When steelhead are in the Grand River

  • October to December: the fall run. Fresh fish follow the salmon in on cold rains and feed on loose eggs.
  • December to February: holdover fish sit in the deepest, slowest pools. Mild days after a thaw are best, where it's open.
  • March and April: the spring run and spawning. Fish move on snowmelt and rain, then spawn on gravel in the upper reaches.
  • Late April and May: drop-backs. Spawned fish feed hard on their way back to Lake Erie.

Reading the Grand River for steelhead

Steelhead hold where the current slows to about walking pace in water a metre or two deep: tailouts above riffles, the inside seam of a bend, slots beside boulders and the soft water below log jams. In cold water they sit deeper and slower; as it warms toward 8 to 10°C they move up into faster runs.

The best days come one or two days after rain, as the river drops and clears from muddy to a green tint. A blown-out river fishes poorly, and gin-clear, low water makes fish spooky, so fish small and light then.

Gear and flies

  • Float fishing: a long rod with a centrepin or spinning reel, a float, and a roe bag, bead or small jig drifted just above bottom. See our float fishing guide.
  • Fly fishing: egg patterns, stonefly and Pheasant Tail nymphs under an indicator, or swung Intruder style streamers in stained water.
  • Spinning: small spoons and inline spinners swung across and down through the runs.

Where to fish

Public places include Caledonia dam and Byng Island Conservation Area (Dunnville). Fish only where access is public or permitted, stay off spawning gravel in spring, and check the stretch rules above before you cast.

Frequently asked

When do steelhead run in the Grand River?

Fresh steelhead push in from Lake Erie with the cold rains of October and November, a few hold in deep pools through winter, and a second run comes in March and April to spawn. Drop-back fish head back to the lake through April and early May.

Can you fish the Grand River for steelhead in winter?

Not on the stretches we cover. They are closed for rainbow trout in mid-winter and reopen with the trout opener on the fourth Saturday in April, or have rules you should check on the zone page.

What is the best bait for steelhead on the Grand River?

Roe bags, single beads and small jigs under a float are the most common Ontario approach. On the fly, egg patterns, stoneflies and swung streamers all work. Match the size and colour to the clarity: bigger and brighter in stained water, smaller in clear water.

How many steelhead can I keep?

Steelhead are rainbow trout under Ontario's rules. In Zones 16, 17 and 20 the limit is two on a sport licence and one on a conservation licence, within the combined trout and salmon limit. Some stretches have their own rules, so check the stretch you fish.
